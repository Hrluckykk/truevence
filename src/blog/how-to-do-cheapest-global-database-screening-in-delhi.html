Why Global Database Screening is Critical

A domestic criminal check is not enough when your business crosses borders, or when you're hiring for regulated sectors like banking, fintech, or international trade. Individuals may appear on global sanctions lists (UN, OFAC, EU, HM Treasury), be classified as PEPs, or have adverse media coverage linking them to financial crimes, terrorism, or corruption. Missing these red flags can lead to severe regulatory penalties, frozen transactions, and reputational damage.

What Databases Does Truevence Cover?

Sanctions & Watchlists

UN Security Council, OFAC (US), EU Consolidated List, UK HMT, Interpol Red Notices, and other regional sanctions bodies.

PEP & RCA

Politically Exposed Persons and their Relatives and Close Associates (RCA) from every country, updated continuously.

Adverse Media

Negative news and media articles from thousands of trusted global sources, covering financial crime, terrorism, human rights violations, and regulatory actions.

Regulatory & Law Enforcement

SEC, FCA, RBI, and other national regulator enforcement lists, debarment lists, and law enforcement wanted notices.

Common Questions About Global Database Screening

Is global database screening only for large multinationals?

No. Even small businesses hiring senior leadership, finance professionals, or individuals who have lived abroad can benefit. If your role involves BFSI compliance, international supply chains, or cross-border data handling, this check is highly recommended.

What happens if a candidate is a false positive match?

Truevence's manual review minimises false positives. If a match is purely name-based with no other corroborating identifiers (age, nationality, location), it is not reported. Only confirmed or strongly correlated matches are flagged.

Can this check replace the AML/CFT screening required by RBI?

It complements it. Regulated entities must perform their own specific AML screening, but Truevence's report provides a documented external check that can be part of your overall compliance framework.

How often should global screening be repeated?

For ongoing employees in sensitive roles, periodic rescreening (annually or as per your compliance calendar) is advisable. Truevence can set up bulk recurring checks at discounted rates.
