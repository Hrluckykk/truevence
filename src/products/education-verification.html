How education verification works

1. Consent & certificate scan

We collect explicit consent and a clear copy of the degree or marksheet.

2. Institution outreach or API

We contact the university or use digital databases like DigiLocker / NAD.

3. Report with authentication

A complete report confirms the degree, major, year of passing, and institution.

Manual Verification

Our team physically or electronically contacts the university registrar, examination controller, or verification cell. This thorough approach includes:

  • Verification of candidate details against the institution's official records
  • Authentication of marksheets, provisional certificates, and convocation degrees
  • Cross‑checking the institution's accreditation status with UGC / AICTE
  • Handling of regional language documents and follow‑up with remote campuses

Manual checks are ideal for older qualifications or institutions that haven't digitised their records.

Digital / API‑based Verification

For modern institutions and government portals, we leverage secure APIs and digital credential platforms to instantly authenticate qualifications:

  • Real‑time verification via DigiLocker, National Academic Depository (NAD), or university portals
  • Tamper‑proof digital certificates validated through blockchain‑backed credentials where available
  • Bulk verification for large batches, ideal for campus hiring and mass recruitment
  • Seamless integration with your ATS or HRMS to automate the credentialing workflow

API checks drastically reduce turnaround time and human error while maintaining full auditability.

Manual vs. Digital / API at a glance

Manual

  • Direct contact with university officials
  • Works with any institution, including older records
  • Slower, especially during peak admission seasons

Digital / API

  • Instant verification through NAD / DigiLocker
  • Highly scalable for bulk checks
  • Limited to institutions that are digitally integrated
