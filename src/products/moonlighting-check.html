How moonlighting check works

1. Consent & UAN

Candidate provides UAN and authorises an EPFO passbook scan.

2. Overlap analysis

Algorithmic scan of EPF data flags overlapping establishments within the same period.

3. Manual verification

If needed, discreet calls to employers confirm whether the second job is active.

EPFO overlap detection + manual validation

Our combination of digital analytics and human investigation provides a conclusive picture.

  • EPFO passbook is parsed automatically — every establishment and date range is extracted
  • Algorithm detects any overlapping membership periods (even by a single day)
  • If overlap is found, a verification officer contacts the second employer to confirm if the job is current or was closed without EPF closure
  • Only confirmed, active dual employments are flagged in the final report

This approach prevents false positives and ensures decisions are based on verified facts.
