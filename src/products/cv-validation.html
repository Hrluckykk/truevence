How CV validation works

1. CV upload & consent

Candidate submits their résumé along with written authorisation for verification.

2. Multi‑source verification

We cross‑check every claim against employers, universities, UAN/EPFO, and public records.

3. Accuracy score & report

A detailed report highlights matches, discrepancies, and a final trust score.

What we validate in the CV

Employment history

Designation, tenure, salary, and reason for leaving cross‑checked with past employers.

Education & qualifications

Degrees, certifications, and year of passing verified with issuing institutions.

Career gaps & explanations

Every gap is identified and matched against the candidate's provided justification.

Skills & professional claims

Key technical and soft skills cross‑referenced with past roles and referee feedback.

How we combine manual & digital checks

Manual deep dive

  • Personal contact with HR and referees
  • Physical document inspection where needed
  • Uncover nuanced feedback not in digital records

Digital / API speed

  • Instant EPFO / UAN data pull
  • Bulk education verification via DigiLocker / NAD
  • Scalable for high‑volume campus hiring

Security & data protection

Consent‑driven

CV analysis begins only after the candidate's explicit, documented permission.

Encrypted report delivery

Reports are shared via secure, access‑controlled channels only.

Data minimized

Full audit trail

Every check is timestamped and linked to consent for regulatory transparency.

Compliance & trust

CV Validation is a comprehensive assessment that combines multiple verification modules, each governed by candidate consent. We follow the principles of data minimization and purpose limitation — only the information necessary to validate the CV is collected and retained. Our process is aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and we maintain detailed audit logs for complete transparency.
