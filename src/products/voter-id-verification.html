Why verify a Voter ID?

Identity proof

A widely accepted government ID for KYC and background checks.

Address validation

Confirm residential address and constituency details.

Fraud prevention

Detect fake or duplicate voter IDs before onboarding.

How Voter ID verification works

1. Consent & EPIC copy

Candidate provides a scan of the voter card and signs consent.

2. Verification run

Manual inspection or digital check via the Electoral Search portal.

3. Report with findings

Report confirms authenticity, matching details, and any discrepancies.

Manual vs. Digital at a glance

Manual Inspection

  • Check security features of physical card
  • Validate name, EPIC number, and photo
  • Slower, requires physical card access

Digital (NVSP Portal)

  • Search electoral roll by EPIC number
  • Confirm name, age, address, and polling station
  • Requires correct EPIC number and state
