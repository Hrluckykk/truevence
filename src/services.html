HOW WE VERIFY

We talk to real people, not just databases.

Every verification goes through a three‑stage funnel: digital forensics, primary source contact, and a second reviewer sign‑off. This means a university clerk, a past manager, or a local police station confirms the data, not just a scanned PDF.

  • Digital forensics – pixel‑level tampering checks on all submitted documents.
  • Direct outreach – phone calls and emails to institutions, not the candidate’s provided contacts.
  • Dual reviewer audit – a second specialist reviews every report before release.
