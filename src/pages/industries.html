Sector · 02

BFSI — where compliance isn't optional.

Banking, financial services and insurance roles carry the highest regulatory exposure of any sector. A weak verification is a compliance event waiting to happen.

Credit & Financial History

CIBIL checks and outstanding default disclosures where the role involves fiduciary responsibility or access to customer funds.

01

Criminal & Court Records

District-level and High Court searches across jurisdictions where the candidate has resided or worked. Financial fraud cases specifically surfaced.

02

AML & Sanction Screening

Screening against global watchlists, PEP databases and sanction registries — mandatory for many BFSI roles under RBI and SEBI guidelines.

03

Regulatory Fit & Proper

Documented evidence trail aligned with RBI, SEBI and IRDAI onboarding norms. Every check leaves an audit-ready record.
