§ 01 — The market signal

The demand already exists. You just need to serve it.

If you run an HR firm, recruitment agency, or staffing company, your clients are already asking about background verification. This is not a trend forecast. It is happening now, in your inbox and on your calls.

The Indian hiring market has changed. Remote work, gig hiring, and rapid scaling have made resume fraud and undisclosed history more common. Industry data shows a 5 to 6 percent gap between what candidates claim and what verification actually confirms. Employers are responding by making background verification a standard part of the hiring process — not an optional extra.

The question is not whether your clients need BGV. The question is whether they buy it from you, or from someone else.

The signal Clients are asking for BGV. The demand is already in your pipeline.
The shift Employers are consolidating vendors — they prefer partners who can do more, not fewer things.
The gap Most HR and recruitment firms do not offer BGV because building it is complex. That is your opening.
The opportunity Add a recurring, high-margin service line without adding operational overhead.

Your clients are not asking "do you do background verification?" They are asking "are you the partner I can trust with everything?"

What partners actually evaluate.

We have spoken with hundreds of potential partners. These are the factors that determine whether they choose a BGV partner — and how we have designed our program to address each one.

1.

Compliance and legal safety.

The DPDP Act makes personal data processing a high-risk area. Partners do not want to inherit liability from a BGV vendor that cuts corners. They need to know: consent is captured correctly, data processing is compliant, audit trails are available. Our platform is built to DPDP requirements.

Data security standards.

You are handing your BGV provider your client's most sensitive data — identity numbers, addresses, work history, criminal records. Partners need to know it is protected. ISO 27001, encryption, access control — these are not optional. Our infrastructure runs on enterprise-grade security.

Coverage and verification quality.

Your clients hire across cities. Partners need to know: does the BGV provider cover tier-2 and tier-3 cities? Can they verify education from slow universities? Your clients are waiting to onboard candidates. Partners need reliable TAT, not vague promises. Clear SLAs by check type, and a defined escalation process for exceptions, are what make a partner program operationally viable.

Clear SLAs TAT by check Escalation path

§ 05 — The economics

How partners make money.

There are two ways partners earn. Most choose the margin model, because it gives full control over pricing and client relationship.

Margin model · Commission model

You set the price. You keep the margin.

The margin model. You buy checks from us at wholesale rates. You bundle them into packages and price them for your client. Your margin is the difference. This is the most common model because it gives you full control over the client relationship and the pricing conversation.

The commission model. You refer the client to us, and we pay you a commission per verification. This works for partners who do not want to handle billing or client-facing delivery.

Why margin beats commission for most partners. When you own the client relationship and the pricing, you also own the retention. The client stays with you, not with the BGV vendor. That is a long-term asset, not a one-time payment.

Typical partner margins range from 20 to 50 percent, depending on volume and how the package is bundled.

§ 06 — Onboarding

How onboarding works.

Partner onboarding is designed to be fast. Most partners go live within 5 to 10 working days.

  • Introductory call. We understand your client base, typical hiring volume, and which checks your clients ask for.
  • Commercial discussion. We share wholesale pricing, discuss package structures, and agree on your margin model.
  • Agreement and documentation. Partner agreement, data processing agreement, and brand usage guidelines are signed.
  • Platform access. You get dashboard access with your branding, user accounts for your team, and API credentials if needed.
  • Training. A short onboarding session on how to submit checks, read reports, and handle candidate queries.
  • Go live. You start offering BGV to your clients. Your dedicated partner manager is available for escalations.
