§ 01 — What it is

What is a BGV reseller program?

A BGV reseller program lets you buy background verification checks at wholesale rates and resell them to your clients — either under your own brand or as part of your existing service offering. You own the client relationship and the pricing conversation. The verification provider handles execution.

In practice, this means you can tell your client "yes, we do background verification" without hiring a verification team, building a technology platform, or managing DPDP compliance yourself. You focus on selling and client management. We focus on running the checks.

For HR firms, recruitment agencies, staffing companies, and consultants in India, this is one of the fastest ways to add a recurring, high-margin service line without capital investment.

What you bring Client relationships and the ability to sell background verification as part of your service.
What we bring Verification execution, technology platform, DPDP compliance, and white label branding.
What you earn The margin between wholesale and retail — typically 20 to 50 percent, controlled entirely by you.
What you avoid No infrastructure, no verification team, no compliance overhead, no platform build.

You do not need to become a background verification company. You need to be the company your clients trust with background verification.

§ 04 — The economics

How resellers make money.

The reseller model is simple: you buy at wholesale, you sell at your price, you keep the difference. Here is what that looks like in practice.

Wholesale in · Your price out · Margin stays with you

Typical reseller margins run 20 to 50 percent per check.

How the margin works. You buy a check at wholesale — say a standard employment verification. You bundle it into a package and price it for your client. The difference is your revenue. You control both the package structure and the client-facing price.

Volume unlocks better rates. As your monthly verification volume grows, your wholesale rate drops. That means either larger margins or more competitive pricing — your choice.

Recurring, not one-time. Background verification is not a one-off purchase. Clients hire continuously. Every new hire is a new verification. That means a reseller relationship generates recurring revenue, not a single transaction.

No infrastructure costs. You do not pay for platform development, verification staff, or compliance infrastructure. Your cost is the wholesale per-check rate. That is the entire cost base.

A reseller with 20 clients running an average of 10 verifications per month is running 200 checks monthly — at a 30 percent margin, that is a meaningful recurring revenue line from an existing client base.

§ 05 — Branding options

White label or co-branded.

Resellers can choose how the service is presented to clients. Both models are supported.

1.

White label.

The client sees only your brand. Reports, dashboards, and communications carry your logo and name. Truevence is invisible to the end client. This is the preferred model for resellers who want to own the full client relationship and build their own BGV brand.

Your brand only Full client ownership Highest margin potential
2.

Co-branded.

The client sees your brand and Truevence's. This model works for resellers who want to leverage Truevence's credibility as a verification provider while still positioning the service as their own. Co-branding can be faster to sell when clients already know and trust the verification provider.

Shared branding Leverages provider trust Faster sales cycle

Most resellers start co-branded and move to white label as their own brand gains recognition. You can switch at any time.

§ 06 — Onboarding

How reseller onboarding works.

Onboarding is designed to get you live quickly. Most resellers are selling within 5 to 10 working days.

  • Discovery call. We understand your client base, typical hiring volume, and which checks your clients ask for most.
  • Commercial agreement. We share wholesale pricing, agree on your margin structure, and sign the reseller agreement.
  • Branding setup. If you choose white label, we configure the dashboard and reports with your logo, colours, and domain.
  • Platform access. You get dashboard access, user accounts for your team, and API credentials if you need integration.
  • Sales enablement. We provide pitch decks, pricing sheets, proposal templates, and a short training session on positioning BGV.
  • Go live. You start selling. Your dedicated reseller manager handles escalations and support.
