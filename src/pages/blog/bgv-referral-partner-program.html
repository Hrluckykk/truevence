§ 01 — What it is

What is a BGV referral program?

A BGV referral partner program pays you a commission every time a company you introduce signs up for background verification services. You do not run the checks, handle billing, or manage client support. You make the introduction. We do the rest. You get paid.

This is the simplest form of partnership. It is designed for people who have relationships with companies that hire — HR professionals, consultants, founders, recruiters, payroll advisors, and anyone else who regularly talks to decision-makers about hiring.

Unlike the reseller program, where you own the client relationship and control pricing, the referral program is a lighter commitment. You introduce, we close, you earn. That is the entire model.

What you do Introduce us to companies that hire — an email, a call, a warm handoff.
What we do Pitch, close, deliver, support. Everything else.
What you earn A referral commission on every client you introduce — one-time or recurring.
What you invest Nothing. No fees, no infrastructure, no delivery cost.

You already know who needs background verification. This program pays you for making the introduction.

§ 04 — What you earn

How much you earn per referral.

Referral commissions are structured in one of two ways, depending on the client and the agreement. Most partners use a combination of both.

One-time · Recurring · Volume-based

Referral commissions typically run 5 to 15 percent of contract value.

One-time referral fee. You receive a fixed percentage of the first contract value when the referred client signs up. This is the simplest structure and works well when you introduce a client once and do not expect to be involved further.

Recurring referral commission. You receive a smaller percentage, but on an ongoing basis, for as long as the client stays. This works well when you are making an ongoing recommendation — for example, an HR consultant who advises the client every quarter.

Volume-based referral bonus. You receive an increasing rate as you refer more clients. Refer one client, earn the base rate. Refer ten, earn a higher rate on all ten.

What determines the rate. Commission depends on the type of client, expected verification volume, and the length of the commitment. We share the exact rate card when you join the program.

Because BGV is recurring — clients verify every new hire — referral income compounds. One good referral can generate commissions for years.

  • Transparent commission structure. The rate card is shared upfront. No hidden deductions, no ambiguity on payout timing.
  • Fast, reliable TAT. Your referral gets delivered. Their hiring does not stall because of slow verification — which protects your reputation.
