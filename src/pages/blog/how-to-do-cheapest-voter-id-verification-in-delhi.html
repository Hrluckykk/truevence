Why Verify a Voter ID?

Many job applicants, especially in blue-collar and entry-level roles, may not have a passport or even a PAN card. The voter ID becomes their primary government-issued photo identity and age proof. Verifying it ensures the person's name, age, and constituency are authentic, and that the document hasn't been forged. It's also a valuable check for address validation, as the constituency link can corroborate the candidate's residential area.

Common Questions About Voter ID Verification

Is voter ID verification legally valid for identity proof?

Yes. The Election Commission of India is a constitutional body, and the Voter ID is an officially recognised identity and age proof document accepted by all government and private entities in India.

What if the candidate's voter ID details don't match?

A mismatch could indicate a fake card, a typo in the provided EPIC number, or that the voter details have been updated (e.g., after marriage). Our report highlights the discrepancy so you can investigate further with the candidate.

Can I verify a voter ID without the EPIC number?

The EPIC number is the most efficient way. Without it, we can attempt a search using name and date of birth, but this is slower and may return multiple matches. We strongly recommend collecting the EPIC number from the candidate.

Does voter ID verification show the photo?

The database query returns demographic details, not the photograph. However, you can always compare the returned details with the physical card's photo to ensure the candidate presenting the card is the same person.
