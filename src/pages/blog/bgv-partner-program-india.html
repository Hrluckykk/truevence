BGV partner program in India: build a revenue stream on background verification.

Background verification is one of the few HR services with recurring demand, predictable margins, and enterprise budget behind it. Our partner program lets you earn from that demand without building the verification infrastructure yourself. Four tracks, transparent commissions, and a delivery team that handles the hard part.

July 2026 9 min read Truevence Editorial Team Filed under Partner Program
§ 01 — Why partner on BGV

Why BGV is a strong revenue partner category.

Most HR services are one-time or project-based. Recruiters earn a fee when a candidate is placed. Consultants earn a fee when a project wraps. Background verification is different: it is triggered every time a company hires, and hiring is continuous. That makes it one of the few HR services that generates recurring revenue from an existing client relationship.

For referral partners, that means commission that repeats every time the client hires. For resellers, it means an ongoing service line that keeps the client engaged between larger transactions. For technology partners, it means a native capability that increases stickiness of their own product.

Recurring demand Every hire triggers a check. Hiring is continuous, so demand is continuous.
Predictable margins Per-check pricing means margin per transaction is transparent and stable.
Enterprise budgets BGV sits under HR and compliance budgets, both of which are protected in downturns.
Zero ops for you We handle verification operations, field agents, technology, and candidate communication.

The best partner businesses are ones where you keep the relationship and hand off the operations. BGV fits that shape exactly.

§ 02 — Partner tracks

Four partner tracks, one BGV infrastructure.

Different partners bring different things. Some bring client relationships, some bring delivery capacity, some bring technology. We have designed the program around four tracks so each partner type gets terms that reflect what they actually contribute.

Referral · Reseller · Technology · Field

Pick the track that matches what you already do well.

Referral partner. You introduce us to HR decision-makers in your network. We handle everything from the first call onwards. You earn 10 to 15 percent of the first-year contract value for every client you refer. Best for HR consultants, recruiters, fractional CHROs, and independent advisors.

Reseller partner. You sell background verification as part of your own service offering, at your own pricing. We provide the delivery infrastructure. You keep the margin between your price and ours. Best for HR services firms, payroll providers, compliance consultancies, and staffing agencies.

Technology partner. You embed our BGV API into your ATS, HRMS, or HR platform. Your clients run verification without leaving your product. Revenue share on every check run through your platform. Best for SaaS companies, HR tech platforms, and applicant tracking systems.

Field verification partner. You provide on-ground verification capacity in one or more Indian cities. We route physical verification work to you. Paid per completed check, with a monthly minimum volume commitment. Best for local agencies with field teams in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

§ 03 — Commission structure

How partner commissions actually work.

Commission structures should be transparent and predictable. Here is how each track is compensated.

Track Commission model Typical range
Referral Percentage of first-year contract value, paid quarterly 10 to 15 percent
Reseller You keep the margin between your price and ours 20 to 40 percent margin
Technology Revenue share on every check run through your platform 15 to 25 percent per check
Field verification Fixed per completed check, paid monthly ₹150 to ₹400 per check by city tier

All commissions are payable on realized revenue, not on invoiced amount, to align interests. Referral and technology partners get a partner dashboard to track their earnings in real time.

§ 04 — Partner benefits

What every partner gets.

The program is designed so that you can focus on what you do well, and we handle everything else. Every partner, regardless of track, gets the same core infrastructure.

  • Dedicated partner manager as your single point of contact for client handoffs and escalations
  • Partner portal with real-time visibility on client activity, commission accrual, and payout status
  • Sales collateral including pitch decks, one-pagers, and case studies you can put your brand on
  • Technical documentation and sandbox access if you are on the technology track
  • Co-branded proposals for enterprise clients, prepared by our team but presented under your brand
  • Quarterly business reviews to review pipeline, performance, and growth opportunities
  • Priority support so your clients get faster responses than standard retail customers
§ 05 — White label

White label BGV for recruitment and staffing firms.

If you run a recruitment or staffing business, white label is often the highest value model. Your clients already trust you with hiring. Adding background verification as a white-labelled service lets you capture more spend per client without needing to build verification capability.

  • Your brand on every report, candidate communication, and status update
  • Your domain on the candidate portal so candidates never see Truevence branding
  • Your pricing so you control the margin between what you charge and what we charge you
  • Our infrastructure including UIDAI authorisation, field agents, university relationships, and court record access
  • Direct client support so your operations team can escalate issues to us without involving the end client
  • Complete commercial confidentiality so your clients never learn who the underlying verification provider is

    • Most white label partners start with a batch of 100 to 200 checks per month and scale from there. There is no minimum volume commitment required to begin.
