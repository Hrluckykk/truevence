§ 01 — Why it matters

Why bulk Aadhaar verification matters.

Whether you need to verify 10 candidates or thousands of employees, Truevence helps businesses streamline identity verification and KYC workflows with technology-enabled solutions designed for speed, accuracy and operational efficiency.

Our API-enabled approach automates verification requests, responses and large-scale screening — so your team stops processing candidates one by one and starts operating at the speed of your hiring.

At 500 candidates a month, manual verification breaks. At 5,000, it stops existing. The right stack runs alongside your ATS, not on top of it.
