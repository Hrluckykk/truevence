§ 04 — Which check when

Which check to run, and when.

Not every role needs every check. Running the right combination for the right hire keeps both cost and turnaround time sensible.

Practical guidance by role type

Right check, right role, right cost.

Gig and blue-collar roles. Identity plus address verification is usually enough. Fast, low cost, and covers the main impersonation risks.

Mid-level office roles. Identity, employment, and education verification together form a solid baseline. Add reference checks when the role is client-facing.

Senior and finance roles. Full package. Identity, employment, education, criminal record, address, and references. This is where a single bad hire can cost the most.

Regulated sectors (BFSI, healthcare, IT). Follow the regulator's minimum requirement, then add checks based on the specific access the role will have.
