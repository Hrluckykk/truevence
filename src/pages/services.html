01The Dream

Imagine hiring with zero surprises.

Why It Matters

You extend an offer, confident that every claim on the resume has been quietly, thoroughly, and confidentially verified. No last-minute doubts. No reputation risk. Just a clean, verified candidate ready to start.

And yet, most offers go out on trust alone.

The Reality

An offer letter goes out. A joining date is set. Somewhere between the interview and the first day at work, a version of the candidate you never met quietly reports for duty.

Inflated tenure. Fabricated degrees. Employers that exist on paper but never existed in reality. One in six Indian resumes carries a material falsehood, and most companies find out eighteen months later, long after the offer letter is signed.

Eight modules exist to close that gap. Run the ones that match the risk profile of the role, and every claim gets a source before it becomes a hire.

02What We Verify

Eight checks. One defensible picture of the person in front of you.

8 Modules
01
Identity Verification
Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID and driving licence checks confirming the candidate is exactly who they say they are, at source.
Prevents identity fraud
02
Employment History
Direct confirmation of role, tenure, salary band and reason for leaving from the past employer's HR team or reporting manager.
Validates real experience
03
Education Verification
Degree and marksheet authentication directly from the issuing university or board. Catches unrecognised institutions dressed up as universities.
Eliminates fake degrees
04
Criminal Record Check
District-level court and police record screening across the candidate's declared jurisdictions. Physical coverage where digital stops.
Protects workplace safety
05
Address Verification
Physical field visit plus geotagged photographic evidence of current and permanent residential address. Digital confirmation where permitted.
Confirms candidate stability
06
Medical Fitness Screening
Health assessments coordinated with certified diagnostic partners, mapped to role-readiness standards for the position you are hiring for.
Ensures role-readiness
07
Reference Checks
Structured conversations with named professional references, scored on performance, integrity and re-hire intent. Insight a form cannot deliver.
Validates soft skills
08
Global and Court Records
Cross-border litigation and regulatory screening for candidates with an international footprint. Early red flags before they become a legal problem.
Cross-border risk coverage
03How We Verify

We talk to real people, not just databases.

Methodology
01
Digital Forensics
Pixel-level tampering checks on every submitted document. Metadata, edits, and provenance verified before any further step runs.
Applied to all documents
02
Direct Outreach
Phone calls and emails to the institution itself, not the contacts the candidate provided. A university clerk, a past manager, a district court registry.
Primary sources only
03
Dual Reviewer Audit
A second specialist who did not run the check reviews every finding for accuracy, completeness and source provenance before sign-off.
Nothing ships unreviewed

"We once detected a forged degree from a university that didn't exist. The candidate had used it for two previous jobs. One direct call to the UGC registry exposed it in 48 hours. That's why we never rely on self-attested documents alone."
