How We Verify

REAL
SOURCES

We talk to real people, not just databases. Every verification goes through a three-stage funnel: digital forensics, primary source contact, and a second reviewer sign-off.

  • ✓ Digital forensics on every document
  • ✓ Direct outreach to institutions
  • ✓ Dual-reviewer audit before release
Verification methodology · Established 2026
01

Digital Forensics

Pixel-level tampering checks on every submitted document. Metadata analysis catches PDF edits and scanned-image manipulation before it reaches a human reviewer.

02

Primary Source Contact

Phone calls and emails to institutions — not the candidate's provided contacts. We verify against the university registrar, the past employer's HR, the court clerk — whoever actually holds the record.

03

Second-Reviewer Audit

A second specialist reviews every report before release. Findings are cross-checked, anomalies are flagged with source-level evidence, and the final sign-off is documented.
