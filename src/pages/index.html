02What We Verify

Eight checks. One defensible picture.

Modules
01

Aadhaar Verification

Confirms the candidate's legal identity against UIDAI demographic data. Returns match or no-match with a confidence band.

→ 02

PAN Verification

Name, date of birth and PAN status validated at NSDL source. Flags dormant, inoperative or mismatched records.

→ 03

Employment Verification

Tenure, designation, CTC band and exit reason confirmed with the past employer's HR or reporting manager.

→ 04

Education Verification

Degree, marksheet and institution records verified at the registrar's office. Detects unrecognised institutions.

→ 05

Criminal Court Check

District-level civil and criminal record search across the candidate's declared address jurisdictions.

→ 06

Address Verification

Physical field visit plus geotagged photographic evidence. Digital residence confirmation where permitted.

→ 07

UAN and EPFO Check

Full employment history reconstructed from EPFO contribution records, independent of the resume.

→ 08

Professional Reference

Structured reference calls with named past managers. Scored on performance, integrity and re-hire intent.
