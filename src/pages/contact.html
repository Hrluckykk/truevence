04Before You Send

Three questions we get every time.

It depends on which modules you run. A basic identity and employment check is significantly cheaper than a full eight-module screen with physical address and district-level court verification. We price per check, with no retainer and no minimum volume. Submit the intake and you get exact numbers back the same business day.
Most checks close in two to five business days. Address and education verifications that require a field visit may run slightly longer. We give you a per-check estimate when the case is scoped, and status updates fire when something changes, not when you chase.
Always. Every check runs on written, documented candidate consent, captured as a signed artifact before the pipeline is armed. DPDP Act 2023 aligned and audit-ready. We do not retain candidate data beyond the case, and we never sell it.
