01Procedure

Five steps. Zero guesswork.

Documented consent first. Primary source verification. Second reviewer before sign-off. Every step is designed to be defensible.

01
Request and ConsentDay 0 · Consent captured
Role and candidate details come in. Documented consent is collected before anything starts, no exceptions. Every check traces back to a signed approval and every artifact is hash-stored on intake.
02
Data CollectionDay 0 to 1 · Missing items flagged
Documents gathered, institutions and past employers identified, references prepared. Missing items flagged within 24 hours, not silently waited on. You always know exactly what is missing and why.
03
Multi Source VerificationDay 1 to 4 · Parallel outreach
Every claim cross-checked against primary sources: court records, universities, employers, UIDAI. Parallel outreach, not sequential waiting. Field agents are dispatched on the same case number.
04
Quality ReviewDay 4 to 5 · Blind second audit
A second reviewer sees raw findings, not the first reviewer's conclusion, before the report is drafted. Confirmation bias is removed by design, not by good intentions.
05
Report DeliveryDay 5 · Source-cited
A clear, structured report delivered to your dashboard, with source citations attached. Ready to act on, ready for audit. Every anomaly documented with the raw evidence behind it.

03Delay Protocol

No silent waiting. Ever.

Some checks take longer because a university is slow or a past employer is unresponsive. Here is how we handle those moments with transparency, not excuses.

01
Proactive Status Updates

You never need to ask. We send updates every 48 hours while a check is in progress, even if there is no new development.

02
Clear Reason for Delay

We tell you exactly why, whether the university is closed for holidays or the past employer HR is on leave, so you can plan around it.

03
Partial Report Option

If one check is stuck but others are complete, we can release a partial report. You decide whether to wait or proceed with what is ready.

04
Fast Track When It Matters

If there is a joining date closing in, tell us upfront. We reprioritise critical checks and reorder the sequence to protect your timeline.

A note from the team

We verify like we are hiring for ourselves.

As a startup ourselves, we approach every verification with the same care we would if the candidate were joining our own team. This mindset shapes our entire process, from how we assign cases to how we communicate with you.

No assembly line. One verifier owns a case end to end, understanding the full context of the candidate. No handoffs, no context loss, no "that's not my job."

Flexible process. Need a specific check done first? We reprioritise and can deliver partial results. Your hiring timeline drives our sequence, not the other way around.

Direct founder access. If something feels off, you are talking to the people who designed the process. No account manager telephone games, no ticket queues.

Continuous improvement. After every case we ask what could have been faster, what could have been clearer. Our process evolves because we treat every client's feedback as product input.
