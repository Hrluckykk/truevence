How It Works

Five steps. Zero guesswork.

Documented consent first. Primary-source verification. Second reviewer before sign-off. Every step is designed to be defensible.

01
Request & Consent
Role and candidate details come in. Documented consent is collected before anything starts — no exceptions. Every check traces back to a signed approval.
02
Data Collection
Documents gathered, institutions and past employers identified, references prepared. Missing items flagged within 24 hours — not silently waited on.
03
Multi-Source Verification
Every claim cross-checked against primary sources — court records, universities, employers, UIDAI. Parallel outreach, not sequential waiting.
04
Quality Review
A second reviewer sees raw findings — not the first reviewer's conclusion — before the report is drafted. Bias removed by design.
05
Report Delivery
A clear, structured report delivered to your dashboard — with source citations attached. Ready to act on, ready for audit.

Behind the scenes

THE
FLOW

From the moment consent lands, four things run in parallel: document forensics, outreach, discrepancy analysis, and blind review. Each has a dedicated owner.

  • → Documents scanned before any call
  • → Outreach runs in parallel, not sequence
  • → Discrepancies investigated, not just flagged
  • → Blind second review before sign-off
Median 2–5 days · 8-module package
01

Document Forensics First

Before any call, submitted documents go through digital analysis. Pixel-level tampering, font mismatches, and template anomalies are flagged within hours. This alone stops 3 out of 10 fraudulent submissions.

02

Parallel Outreach

Our team contacts universities, employers, and references simultaneously — not one after another. If a university doesn't respond in 48 hours, we escalate to an alternate contact or a physical visit.

03

Discrepancy Deep-Dive

When a claim doesn't match source data, we don't just flag it. We investigate: clerical error, misunderstanding, or intentional? The context matters — and it changes the recommendation.

04

Blind Second Review

A second reviewer sees only raw findings — not the first reviewer's conclusion. This eliminates confirmation bias before the final report is drafted.

05

Report Sign-Off

Every finding source-cited, every anomaly documented, every check traceable back to its origin. The signed report is audit-ready the moment it lands.

Honest Timelines

No silent waiting. Ever.

Some checks take longer because a university is slow or a past employer is unresponsive. Here's how we handle those moments — with transparency, not excuses.

Proactive Status Updates

You never need to ask. We send updates every 48 hours while a check is in progress, even if there's no new development.

01

Clear Reason for Delay

We tell you exactly why — "university closed for holidays," "past employer's HR is on leave" — so you can plan around it. No vague "in progress" placeholders.

02

Partial Report Option

If one check is stuck but others are complete, we can release a partial report. You decide whether to wait for the full picture or proceed with what's ready.

03

Fast-Track When It Matters

If there's a joining date closing in, tell us upfront. We reprioritise critical checks and reorder the sequence to protect your timeline.
