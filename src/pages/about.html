01Why We Started

Background verification should feel like a conversation, not a transaction. We built it that way, from day one.

We founded Truevence because we saw HR teams stuck between slow, impersonal vendors and the fear of a bad hire. So we built a company where every client gets a direct line to the people doing the work.

No call centres. No ticket queues. Just a founder-led team that treats your candidate's background as if it were our own hire.

02Our Creed

Four principles that guide every verification we run.

i.
Confidentiality FirstArticle i · Data handling
Candidate data never leaves our secure environment. We share only the final report, never the raw documents, unless a specific check requires it.
ii.
Consent-Led ProcessArticle ii · Legal and ethical
We never start a check without documented candidate consent. It is the law, and it is also the right thing to do.
iii.
Responsive TurnaroundArticle iii · Communication
You get a dedicated point of contact who updates you without you having to ask. We are small enough that no case gets lost.
iv.
Founder-Led OversightArticle iv · Accountability
Every process is designed and reviewed by the founders. When you email, you reach the people who built the system.

04Culture of Precision

We are obsessed with getting the small things right.

A single digit wrong in a date, a misspelt name, an unchecked court jurisdiction. These are not minor errors when someone's career is at stake. Our team follows a simple internal mantra: "Verify every fact as if the candidate were your next colleague."

This means cross-referencing, calling twice when one answer seems off, and never settling for a "maybe."
