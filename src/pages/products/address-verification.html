Why verify address?

Risk mitigation

Flag addresses in high‑risk areas or inconsistencies across documents.

Regulatory compliance

Meet KYC norms for financial, telecom, and other regulated sectors.

How address verification works

1. Consent & details

We collect signed consent and the candidate's current and permanent addresses.

2. Field visit or digital check

An agent visits the location or we use digital authentication (e.g., Aadhaar address).

3. Report with evidence

A detailed report with photos, GPS coordinates, and neighbour confirmation (if needed).

Physical Field Verification

Our nationwide network of field agents personally visits the candidate's address to confirm its existence and occupancy. They collect:

  • Geo‑tagged photographs of the property (exterior and interior, with consent)
  • Neighbourhood confirmation (discreetly, without revealing the purpose)
  • Electricity bill or rental agreement cross‑verification
  • Detailed observations about the locality and any discrepancies

Physical visits are ideal for sensitive roles, large‑scale blue‑collar hiring, or when digital records are insufficient.

Digital Address Verification

Leverage technology for instant, paperless address checks without a physical visit:

  • Aadhaar e‑KYC with address match (consent‑based, UIDAI‑compliant)
  • Bank statement or utility bill upload and auto‑verification through OCR
  • Video verification call where the candidate shows their current location
  • Seamless integration with onboarding portals for a fully digital flow

Digital checks are ideal for remote workers, quick‑turnaround gig hiring, and scaling onboarding.

Physical vs. Digital at a glance

Physical Field Visit

  • Agent visits the address in person
  • Photo, GPS, and neighbour confirmation
  • Slower (agent scheduling & travel)

Digital

  • Instant via Aadhaar e‑KYC or video call
  • No travel, fully remote
  • Limited to candidates with digital documents

Compliance & trust

Every address verification is conducted with explicit candidate consent. Our field agents are trained in privacy‑first protocols — they never disclose the purpose to neighbours or landlords without permission. All data is stored temporarily and purged after the verification window. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and maintain audit trails for every step.
