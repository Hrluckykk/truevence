02How It Works

From consent to signed report, in three quiet steps.

Consent first, always. Then the digital route runs. Then you get a defensible report. No surprises, no hidden steps.

Signed consent is collected and hash stored with a timestamp before any query leaves the pipeline. Never bundled with employment terms. The candidate's current and permanent addresses are captured alongside.

04When Digital Isn't Enough

Field visits for the edge cases.

Digital works for the majority of candidates. But some roles and some addresses genuinely need an agent on the ground — blue-collar hiring at scale, sensitive roles, or an address that cannot be confirmed against any government record.

For those, our nationwide field network takes over. Same consent, same audit trail, same signed report. Just physical instead of digital.

Geo-tagged photographs

Exterior and interior photos of the property, time-stamped and location-stamped on the agent's device.

Neighbour confirmation

Discreet occupancy confirmation from neighbours, without revealing the purpose of the visit.

Utility bill cross-check

Electricity bill, rental agreement or society records cross-referenced against the claimed address.

Locality observations

Agent notes on access, safety, building condition and any visible discrepancy with the candidate's claims.

06Security and Data Protection

Handled like a regulatory obligation, because it is.

Truevence operates as a Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025. Every check, digital or physical, is designed to survive a regulatory audit.

01

Consent first, unbundled.

Standalone consent, timestamped before any digital query or field visit. Never bundled with employment terms. Retained for the statutory period and linked to every downstream check.

02

Zero biometric storage.

Aadhaar e-KYC runs consent-based against UIDAI. Core biometric data is never stored, never logged and never cached. Only the demographic match response is processed and retained.

03

Encrypted end to end.

All evidence — photographs, GPS logs, documents, reports — is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Retained only for the contractual period, then purged.

04

Discreet field protocols.

Field agents never reveal the purpose of a visit to neighbours or landlords. No branding, no disclosure beyond what consent permits. Same privacy-first standard applies to every digital interaction.

05

Audit logs, end to end.

Every step — consent capture, query, evidence upload, analyst review, sign-off — is timestamped and linked to the consent record. The complete trail is available for the life of the retention window.
