How Digital KYC works

1. Consent & ID upload

Candidate grants permission and uploads Aadhaar and PAN via a secure link.

2. Real‑time verification

APIs validate Aadhaar and PAN instantly; a live selfie confirms liveness.

3. KYC report

A consolidated report with match results, liveness score, and audit trail.

API‑based KYC

Integrate our APIs directly into your platform. Instant, silent, and fully automated.

  • Real‑time Aadhaar and PAN validation from authorised UIDAI/NSDL gateways
  • OCR extraction of ID documents for autofill
  • Zero manual intervention — ideal for high‑volume, self‑serve flows
  • Response in milliseconds with a simple JSON payload

Perfect for fintech apps, digital lending, and instant account opening.

Video‑based KYC (Assisted)

A trained agent verifies the candidate over a live video call, recording the session for compliance.

  • Government‑approved for full KYC under RBI's video KYC guidelines
  • Agent confirms the candidate's face matches the ID documents in real time
  • Geo‑tagged, encrypted recording stored as audit proof
  • Works even when Aadhaar biometric authentication is not possible

Ideal for regulated entities that need full KYC without physical branch visits.
