How police verification assistance works

1. Consent & document collection

We gather the candidate's consent, ID proofs, address documents, and photographs.

2. Application & liaison

We submit the application to the jurisdictional police station and track its progress.

3. Clearance report

We deliver the police clearance certificate or explain any adverse observations.

Compliance & trust

Police verification is conducted strictly within the legal framework of the Criminal Procedure Code and respective state police guidelines. We act as an intermediary only — the final clearance is issued by the jurisdictional police. Candidate data is handled with strict confidentiality and purged after the process, in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
