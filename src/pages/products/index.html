The Philosophy

Most BGV vendors sell packages.
We sell individual checks — so you pay for what the role actually needs, not what a sales rep wants to sell.

Truevence operates as a modular catalogue. Every module stands alone, has its own price, and can be combined with any other module. There is no "silver / gold / platinum" tier. There is no per-seat licence. There is no minimum volume commitment.

Below, twenty-plus modules are arranged into six categories. Each entry is what it is: a specific check, against a specific source, with a specific turnaround. Read it like a spec sheet, or pick what the role needs and send it to us.
