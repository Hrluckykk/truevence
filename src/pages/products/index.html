00The Philosophy

Most BGV vendors sell packages. We sell individual checks, so you pay for what the role actually needs.

Truevence operates as a modular catalogue. Every module stands alone, has its own price, and can be combined with any other module. There is no silver, gold or platinum tier. No per-seat licence. No minimum volume commitment.

Below, twenty plus modules are arranged into six categories. Each entry is what it is: a specific check, against a specific source, with a specific turnaround. Read it like a spec sheet, or pick what the role needs and send it to us.

02Category

Hybrid 2 to 4 days HR plus PF plus reference triangulation

Moonlighting Check

TAT 3 to 5 daysSource EPFO plus open web
