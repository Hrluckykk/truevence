01Why Verify PAN

Four lines on a tax filing that nobody can fake.

PAN is issued once, tied to a single person, and referenced by every financial institution the candidate has touched. That makes it the single best anchor for financial identity — and the single best place to catch a fraud early.

A candidate can change their CV. They can edit a payslip. They can even borrow a friend's Aadhaar. The one thing they cannot quietly change is the number the tax department already knows them by.
01

Prevents financial fraud

Fake, duplicate and borrowed PANs get caught before the candidate enters payroll, vendor systems or a fiduciary role.

02

TDS and Form 16 correctness

The PAN on file must match the one in payroll, or every tax filing downstream will misalign and flag.

03

Duplicate PAN detection

One person, one PAN. A second PAN is a hard signal of attempted identity manipulation — used for loan fraud and round tripping.

04

Regulatory KYC anchor

BFSI, NBFC, fintech, mutual funds and insurance all require PAN as part of mandatory KYC. A clean PAN verification is the first gate.

05

Cross-references the rest

PAN anchors the name and DOB used across UAN, bank account, credit and MCA checks. Verified once, it strengthens every downstream module.

06

Bulk at zero friction

API checks complete in under a second — same per-check rigour for one candidate or one thousand, at mass hiring volumes.

02How It Works

Consent. Check. Report. In three ruled stages.

Same three stages whether we run the check by hand or through the API. The stage names stay constant; only the underlying method changes.

01
Day 0 · Consent

Signed consent, timestamped

Explicit candidate consent is collected and hash stored before any query leaves the pipeline. Never bundled with employment terms.

02
Same day · Verification

Check runs against source

Manual inspection of the original card by a trained officer, or an API query against the Income Tax database. Both routes hit the primary source, not a reseller record.

03
Signed · Report

Audit-ready output

Match status, confidence band and masked source values in a signed report, reviewed by a second analyst before it reaches your dashboard.

03Two Routes

Manual or API. Both hit the source.

Same underlying database. Two very different operating shapes. Physical inspection for the roles that need eyes on the card. API for the volumes that cannot wait.

Route A · Manual Officer reviewed · 1 to 2 hrs

Handled by a trained officer.

Physical inspection of the original PAN card with a documented audit trail. The card is checked against known genuine patterns and against the source database through official channels.

✓Hologram, lamination quality and security features verified by hand.
✓Number format confirmed as five letters, four digits, one letter, with a valid checksum.
✓Photo and signature matched against the candidate's other submitted documents.
✓Cross-referenced against the Income Tax database via official channels.
×Not instant — turnaround depends on officer scheduling and physical document movement.
Route B · API Real time · under 1 second

Delivered in under a second.

For digital-first and volume-driven organisations, our API integration connects directly to the Income Tax PAN verification service. Real-time match, no manual handling.

✓Real-time validation of PAN number, name and date of birth against the source.
✓Instant match or no-match with masked source data and confidence band.
✓TLS 1.3 in transit, AES 256 at rest. Zero sensitive data retained beyond the check window.

04Security and Data Protection

Handled like a regulatory obligation, because it is.

Truevence operates as a Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025. Every PAN check is designed to survive a regulatory audit.

01 · CONSENT

Consent, unbundled

Standalone, timestamped consent before any query. Never bundled with employment terms. Retained for the statutory period.

02 · TRANSMISSION

Encrypted end to end

TLS 1.3 in transit, AES 256 at rest. All API communication is signed and verified. No plaintext leaves the pipeline.

03 · STORAGE

No permanent storage

PAN data is held only for the verification window, then purged. Only the match response and confidence band are retained.

04 · AUDIT

Audit logs, end to end

Every step — consent, query, response, sign-off — is timestamped and linked to the consent record for the full retention window.
