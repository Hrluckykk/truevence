How MCA director check works

1. Consent & DIN

Candidate provides written consent and Director Identification Number (DIN) or name.

2. MCA database query

We search the MCA master data for all active and past directorships.

3. Detailed report

Report lists all companies, incorporation dates, and status of each directorship.

Instant MCA database search

We directly query the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) master data portal, which is the authoritative source for directorship information in India.

  • Retrieve all active and past directorships using DIN or PAN/name match
  • View company CIN, date of incorporation, registered address, and status
  • Identify disqualifications, struck‑off companies, and dormant entities
  • Cross‑reference with other databases to detect shell company associations

The check is entirely digital, with results typically available within minutes.
