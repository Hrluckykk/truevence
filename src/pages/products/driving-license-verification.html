Why verify a driving license?

Safety & liability

Avoid legal risk by ensuring only licensed drivers operate company vehicles.

Fraud prevention

Detect fake, expired, or duplicate licenses before they cause damage.

Regulatory compliance

Required under Motor Vehicles Act for transport and commercial driving roles.

How driving license verification works

1. Consent & DL details

Candidate submits a copy of the DL and written consent for verification.

2. Database or manual check

We query the state transport department portal or manually inspect the physical card.

3. Report with validity

Report confirms DL authenticity, category, validity dates, and endorsements.

Manual vs. API at a glance

Manual Inspection

  • Physical card security features check
  • Works for all states, even without APIs
  • Slower, requires document submission

API (Parivahan)

  • Instant DL validation via Parivahan
  • Returns category, validity, endorsements
  • Limited to states integrated with Parivahan

Security & data protection

Consent‑first

DL verification only with candidate's written consent.

Encrypted channels

API calls use TLS; physical copies are scanned and encrypted.

Minimal retention

DL data is deleted after report generation.

Audit logs

All verifications are timestamped and linked to consent.
