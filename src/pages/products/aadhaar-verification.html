05DPDP and Data Protection

Handled like a regulatory obligation, because it is.

Truevence operates as a Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025. Every Aadhaar check is designed to survive a regulatory audit.

01 · CONSENT

Consent first, unbundled

Standalone consent, timestamped before any check. Never bundled with employment terms. Retained for the statutory period.

02 · MINIMISATION

Data minimisation

Only the checks relevant to the specific role are run. Financial history checks limited to BFSI and senior roles with justification.

03 · BIOMETRICS

Zero biometric storage

Core biometric data is never stored, never logged and never cached. Only the demographic match response is processed.

04 · VENDORS

Signed DPAs, end to end

Every API provider, database vendor and sub processor operates under a signed Data Processing Agreement with Truevence.

06Data Retention

Hard copy lifecycle, fully logged.

Under DPDP Rules 2025, processing logs must be retained for a minimum of one year. Here is the exact path a physical Aadhaar copy takes once it reaches us.

Day 0
Hard copy received

Physical Aadhaar copy received at our secure facility. Entry logged with date, time and the receiving officer's ID.

Within 24 hours
Digitisation and secure storage

Document scanned into our encrypted portal. Original hard copy moved to locked storage under a chain of custody record.

Within 7 days
Hard copy destruction

Physical copies shredded and destroyed. Certificate of destruction issued with date, method and responsible person recorded.

1 to 3 years
Digital record retention

Digitised records and processing logs retained for the period defined in the client contract and applicable law. Minimum one year for logs under DPDP.

On expiry
Secure erasure

Data erased once purpose is served and the retention period expires. Erasure logged and certified so the file is defensibly closed.
