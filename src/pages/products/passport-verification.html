Why verify a passport?

Identity assurance

Passports are a government‑issued, globally accepted identity document.

Fraud detection

Identify altered or forged passports before onboarding.

Compliance

Needed for roles involving foreign travel, immigration, or visa sponsorship.

How passport verification works

1. Consent & copy

Candidate provides a scan of the passport and signed consent.

2. Official verification

We check details through passport seva or physical inspection.

3. Validity report

Report confirms authenticity, expiry, and matching personal details.

Manual vs. Digital at a glance

Manual Inspection

  • Verify security features (hologram, MRZ)
  • Check lamination, binding, and photo integrity
  • Requires physical document submission

Digital (Passport Seva)

  • Real‑time verification via official portal
  • Instantly validate passport number, name, DOB
  • Requires candidate's passport details and consent

Compliance & trust

Passport verification is performed under the candidate's consent and in alignment with the Passport Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not retain passport images beyond the verification lifecycle and ensure all data is transmitted securely.
