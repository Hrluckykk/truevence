THE DAY‑BY‑DAY FLOW

What happens after you hit "submit."

The five steps above are the skeleton. Here's the living, breathing process that unfolds each day.

1

Document Forensics First

Before any call, submitted documents go through digital analysis. Pixel‑level tampering, font mismatches, and template anomalies are flagged within hours. This alone stops 3 out of 10 fraudulent submissions.

2

Parallel Outreach

Our team contacts universities, employers, and references simultaneously – not one after another. If a university doesn't respond in 48 hours, we escalate to an alternate contact or a physical visit.

3

Discrepancy Deep‑Dive

When a claim doesn't match source data, we don't just flag it. We investigate: clerical error, misunderstanding, or intentional? The context matters.

4

Blind Second Review

A second reviewer sees only raw findings, not the first reviewer's conclusion. This eliminates confirmation bias before the final report is drafted.

HOW WE HANDLE DELAYS

Honest timelines, no silent waiting.

Some checks take longer because a university is slow or a past employer is unresponsive. Here's how we handle those moments – with transparency, not excuses.

•

Proactive Status Updates

You never need to ask. We send updates every 48 hours while a check is in progress, even if there's no new development.

•

Clear Reason for Delay

We tell you exactly why – "university closed for holidays," "past employer's HR is on leave" – so you can plan around it.

•

Partial Report Option

If one check is stuck but others are complete, we can release a partial report. You decide whether to wait or proceed.
