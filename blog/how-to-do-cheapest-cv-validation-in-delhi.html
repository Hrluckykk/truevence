What is CV Validation and Why Should You Use It?

CV validation is not a watered-down check; it is a focused, high-impact verification bundle that targets the most commonly falsified parts of any resume. While a full background verification digs deep into every past employment, every degree, and criminal records, CV validation asks three core questions: Is the person's identity real? Do they actually live where they claim? And is their most recent job or highest qualification genuine?

This approach catches the majority of dishonest applications at a fraction of the cost. It is especially useful for high-volume recruitment where you cannot afford to run ₹500+ checks on every applicant but also cannot risk hiring someone with a completely fake profile.

Common Questions About CV Validation

Can I customise the checks in the CV validation bundle?

Yes. While the ₹150 standard bundle covers identity, address, and last employment/education, you can add optional checks like UAN verification, criminal court search, or driving license verification at a small additional cost. Contact our team for a tailored quote.

What if the candidate has no previous employment record?

For freshers, we default to verifying the highest educational qualification. This confirms the candidate's degree or diploma is genuine, which is often the most important piece of information on a fresh graduate's CV.

How do I know if a candidate failed CV validation?

Our report clearly flags any discrepancies. An identity mismatch, an undeliverable address, or a previous employer who denies the candidate worked there will all be highlighted in red. We also add notes from the verification officer to give context.

Is candidate consent required for CV validation?

Absolutely. We collect a signed consent form before initiating any checks. This is both a legal requirement and an ethical practice. A candidate who refuses consent is automatically flagged.
