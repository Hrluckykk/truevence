Why Address Verification is Non-Negotiable

A candidate's residential address is more than just a formality. For positions that involve handling company property, visiting client sites, or working remotely, a verified address provides a safety net. In many industries, it's also a regulatory requirement. This deters impersonation, identifies potential flight risks, and validates the candidate's background beyond the office.
